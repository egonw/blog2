Last month I reported a [A list of things I miss in CiteULike](http://chem-bla-ics.blogspot.com/2010/09/list-of-things-i-miss-in-citeulike.html)
and the developers have not been sitting still. Only *one* of the things they
started to adopt is [RDFa](http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml-rdfa-primer/) in the [CiteULike
web interface](http://www.citeulike.org/user/egonw/article/7812280). Of course,
you will not see this in the browser, unless you use something like the [RDFaDev
plugin in Firefox <i class="fa-solid fa-recycle fa-xs"></i>](https://chem-bla-ics.linkedchemistry.info/2010/07/16/rdfadev-htmlrdfa-development-with.html):

![](/assets/images/culRDFa.png)

Cheers to the [CiteULike developers team](http://blog.citeulike.org/)!
