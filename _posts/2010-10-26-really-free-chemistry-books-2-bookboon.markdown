About a year ago I wrote about [free chemistry books <i class="fa-solid fa-recycle fa-xs"></i>](https://chem-bla-ics.linkedchemistry.info/2009/09/17/really-free-chemistry-books.html).
No, not illegal copied books, but really free books (though, not necessarily
Open). Actually, the books I discussed last year, those are out of copyright,
as they are old. But, just today I ran into an advertisement for free books
by [bookboon.com](http://bookboon.com/) (twitter:[bookboon](http://twitter.com/#!/bookboon)),
and these are *new* books. I have not browsed them yet, but you can download
textbook PDFs from these sections: [biology](http://bookboon.com/us/textbooks/biology),
[chemistry](http://bookboon.com/us/textbooks/chemistry), [chemical engineering](http://bookboon.com/us/textbooks/chemical),
[math](http://bookboon.com/us/textbooks/mathematics), [nanotechnology](http://bookboon.com/us/textbooks/nano),
and several more. I do not know about the quality of these books yet, and they
author names do not immediately ring a bell. Does anyone have experience with
these books?
