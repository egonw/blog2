The next few days there will be a disturbance of the force: [#pmrhack](http://search.twitter.com/search?q=%23pmrhack)
and [#pmrsymp](http://search.twitter.com/search?q=%23pmrsymp). Because I have
an important meeting early next week in my new position, I am unable to attend
these events. The first is a symposium organize in honor of [Peter Murray-Rust](http://blogs.ch.cam.ac.uk/pmr/),
called [Visions of a Semantic Molecular Future Symposium](http://www-ucc.ch.cam.ac.uk/news/visions-semantic-molecular-future-symposium-17th-january-2011),
which attracts over 100 people! Maybe Open Source cheminformatics *has* taken
off ;) (Or, maybe it's just people who love to see how it has not.)

The second event is the [hackfest/unconference](http://www-pmr.ch.cam.ac.uk/wiki/Visions_of_a_%28Semantic%29_Molecular_Future)
held this weekend. That will be at least as much fun as the symposium. Now,
I have plenty of house cleaning to do of our old house (we just moved), but
will try to virtually attend that as much as possible too. Mind you, I expect
to use many of the technologies the Blue Obelisk develops in my new project.

Anyways, I really regret not being able to attend, and am happy that [Noel](http://baoilleach.blogspot.com/)
(thanx!) has taken over advertising some of the [Blue Obelisk](http://blueobelisk.org)
projects I worked on: [Bioclipse](http://planet.bioclipse.net/) and the [CDK](http://cdk.sf.net).
(It seems that [Christoph](http://www.steinbeck-molecular.de/steinblog/) will
not be attending the meeting either...)

This afternoon the people in Peter's group are working out the technical details
for live streaming of the event(s), and I am really looking forward to that!
Good luck to all, and hope to **see** you the next days ;)
