Fulfilling a request by [Daniel](http://de.linkedin.com/pub/daniel-mietchen/0/a88/25b),
following [Antony's title](http://www.chemconnector.com/chemunicating/fill-in-the-wikipedia-survey-and-help-our-community.html),
and adding a random link to [a Wikipedia molecule](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goserelin)
just to see it show up in [Chemical blogspace](http://cb.openmolecules.net/)
now that I fixed the InChI detection, I hereby invite every scientist to fill
out [this questionnaire](http://survey.nitens.org/?sid=21693), surveying how
scientists use and do not use Wikipedia to spread knowledge.

As a nice spin-off if gives you a star rating for how well you are into the
social science networks:

![](/blog/assets/images/showoff.png)

I got eleven stars, but that should not surprise you.
