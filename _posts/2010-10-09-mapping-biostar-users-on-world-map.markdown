just like the [Blue Obelisk eXchange](http://blueobelisk.shapado.com/). Neil
and Pierre have an ongoing struggle to gain the most karma, requiring Pierre
to put in a [formal complaint](https://friendfeed.com/yokofakun/6f72029c/please-stop-asking-questions-on-while-i-m)
against people posting questions when he is asleep (the whole 3 hours). So,
I coined to idea of mapping all [BioStar users](http://biostar.stackexchange.com/users)
on a Google Map. Neil picked it up, and had combined his coding skills with
the various Open API, Open Standards, and Open Source solution, to [come up
only hours later](http://nsaunders.wordpress.com/2010/10/09/biostar-users-of-the-world-unite/)
with [this map](http://www.zeemaps.com/185997). Here are the BioStar users from
