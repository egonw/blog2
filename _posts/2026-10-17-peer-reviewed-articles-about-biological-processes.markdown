Earlier this year I [blogged](https://chem-bla-ics.linkedchemistry.info/2026/02/22/where-do-the-wikipathways-come-from.html) about a few
collaborations where [WikiPathways](https://wikipathways.org/) content has been created and/or curated. I mentioned that
*there are pathways in WikiPathways supported by a full paper, but I will leave that for a later moment*.

Well, it has been a busy year, but that moment has arrived.

Almost every year since the start of WikiPathways a paper was published described mostly one and sometimes a few pathways. Here
is an overview:

That list turned out to be quite a bit longer than I expected. I only knew of a handful of articles describing newly created/curated
pathways. This is positive. If peer review works well, then these pathways, as part of these articles have been peer reviewed well too.
It is reasonable assume that peer review will not be ideal (it is not), but I even found an example of an article describing a WikiPathways
that was retracted, and so has been the WikiPathways itself.

Did I miss an article? Let me know, and I will add it to this list!
