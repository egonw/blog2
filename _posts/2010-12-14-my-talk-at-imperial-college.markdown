Yesterday I was guest with [Judy](http://www1.imperial.ac.uk/medicine/people/j.fonville07/)
at the [Department of Surgery & Cancer](http://www1.imperial.ac.uk/surgeryandcancer/)
at [Imperial College](http://www1.imperial.ac.uk/), where I presented the what
I am working on. As I met up in a pub with Ron (my former PhD supervisor, who
now works on metabolomics at the [Istituto Agrario San Michele all'Adige](http://www.iasma.it/)
in Italy) the night before, the presentation does share many slides from previous
talks, but also adds a few new ones.

[![](/blog/assets/images/odosos_imperial2010.png)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3597909)

It was great talking to Judy about her PhD research on NMR in metabolomics,
and [Tim](http://www1.imperial.ac.uk/medicine/people/t.ebbels/) about further
work in this research. I was interesting to learn that here too they have problems
in metabolite identification quick like those when analyzing \*C/MS data, and
was really happy to hear he is in contact with [Christoph](http://www.steinbeck-molecular.de/steinblog/)
about an Open repository for metabolomics data.

Another 'new' slide was one advertising the yesterday launched [Open Research
Computation](http://www.openresearchcomputation.com/) which I'll blog about
in more detail later today.
